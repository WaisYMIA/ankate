Why Ankate exists

HR should strengthen the business, not slow it down.

We bring together workforce development, HR administration, compliance, recruitment and advisory so leaders can build stronger teams while reducing avoidable HR risk.

Our work rests on professional standards, ethical HR practice and continuous development, with solutions shaped around the realities of growing organisations in Kenya.

Questions clients ask

A few direct answers.

Do you only work with large companies?

No. Most of our work is with growing organisations that do not yet have a full HR team.

Will I always deal with you?

Ankate is founder-led, so you have direct access to me. Where support from others is needed, I stay accountable for the work.

Can you work with us outside Nairobi?

Yes. We serve organisations across Kenya, remotely and on site by agreement.

Where we work

Serving organisations across all of Kenya.

Our office is in Ruiru, and we support employers in every part of Kenya, from Nairobi and Mombasa to Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and smaller towns.

Advisory, payroll, recruitment support and outsourced HR can be delivered remotely. Training, workplace reviews and on-site support are arranged by agreement. Tell us where you are and we will propose the practical way to work together.
